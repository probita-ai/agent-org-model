Agent platform · organization model

A company of humans and agents, from board to worker

Every box is a seat that a human or an agent can fill. Each leader gets a share of capacity from above and splits it among the seats below. Leaders can hire agents onto probation, bench the ones that underperform, and rehire them later. Every agent seat has a human overseer who can see it, pause it and undo its work.

Seats and oversight

Every box is a seat that a human or an agent can fill

A seat holds the authority, the budget share and the reports. Who fills it is one field, so handing a seat to an agent or taking it back changes nothing else. Agent seats carry an autonomy level that rises as the scorecard earns it, and every agent seat has a named human overseer with a backup.

L1 · Manual

A human fills the seat and makes every decision. Agents only do assigned tasks.

L2 · Suggest

The agent proposes hires, benches and rehires. The overseer approves each one.

L3 · Act and report

The agent acts within its limits. The overseer reviews afterwards and can undo.

L4 · Autonomous

The agent acts within its limits. Only exceptions reach a human.

What an overseer can always do

SeeLive status, spend, scorecard and recent decisions
PauseStop one agent, a crew, a division or everything
Roll backUndo any applied change from its stored before-state
OverrideChange any decision; it counts against the agent's score
Take backMove a seat from an agent to a human at once
AuditSearch the ledger for who decided what, and who approved it
How the percentages work

Capacity flows down, one split at a time

Capacity is the money, tokens and concurrent job slots a unit may use. Each leader gets a share of its parent's capacity and splits it among its reports. Whatever a leader doesn't assign stays in its reserve. Here is one monthly budget traced from the board to a single worker agent.

    Lifecycle

    From hire to bench and back

    1. Catalog

      Agent definitions live in the registry. A lead picks one that its authority allows.

    2. Probation

      Shadow mode at 0% capacity. Its outputs are compared with the crew's, and nothing it does takes effect.

    3. Active

      Gets a real share of capacity and its work takes effect, within its permits and checklists.

    4. Review

      HR Agent scores it on a schedule: success rate, cost per job, checklist pass rate, human overrides.

    Promote

    More capacity or a bigger role

    Lead raises its share, or the executive makes it a crew lead.

    Retrain

    New prompt, skills or model

    A change set updates its definition, then it goes back to probation.

    Bench

    Fired, but kept

    Capacity drops to 0 and access is revoked. Its history stays, so it can be rehired.

    Retire

    Removed for good

    Needs a human's approval. The ledger keeps its record.

    Configuration

    A seat is just config

    seat: platform-lead
occupant: { type: agent, agent: lead-agent@v2 }
autonomy: L2                # suggest; overseer approves
overseer: { user: priya, backup: sam }
reports_to: cto
capacity:
  from_parent: 60%          # of the CTO's share
  allocations:
    coder: 40%
    tester: 35%
    reviewer: 25%
authority:
  can_hire: [catalog:engineering/*]  # into probation only
  can_bench: true
  can_retire: false             # a human approves
  max_agents: 6
  approve_change_sets: { max_risk: low }
review:
  every: 7d
  scorecard: [success_rate, cost_per_job,
              checklist_pass, human_overrides]
  bench_if: { score_below: 60, reviews: 2 }
  probation: { mode: shadow, min_jobs: 20,
               promote_if: "agreement >= 85%" }
    • Humans own the topThe board sets the total budget and hard limits. No agent can raise its own budget or one above it.
    • Authority only flows downA leader can hire, bench or reallocate only inside its own subtree and within its permits.
    • Firing is reversibleBenching takes away capacity and access but keeps the agent's definition and history. Only retirement is permanent.
    • Every move is a change setReallocations, hires and firings are proposed, policy-checked, approved at the right level and written to the ledger.
    • No unwatched agentsAn agent seat can't be activated without a named human overseer and a backup.
    • The off switch is plain softwarePausing never depends on an agent cooperating, and agents can't lower their own oversight.
    • Probation before powerNew and rehired agents prove themselves in shadow mode before they get real capacity.
